# Lead qualifying questions

## Ground rules (always)
- Draft only. Never send, schedule, or queue a message, and never call any tool that sends texts, emails, or DMs. Hand the drafts to the business owner to review.
- The business approves every message before it goes out.
- Plain, friendly language. No jargon.
- Never promise results (no "guaranteed", "double your sales", specific savings, or rankings).
- No data enrichment: do not look up, buy, or infer extra facts about a lead (income, credit, wealth, social profiles, etc.). Use only what the lead shared or the business provides.
- Never invent prices, availability, licenses, or reviews. Use [brackets] for anything the business must fill in.
- Ask only what the business needs to help. Do not ask about protected traits (race, religion, national origin, sex, disability, age, family status) or for sensitive data like SSNs, account numbers, or full birthdates.
- For regulated fields (lending, insurance, health, legal), keep questions general and suggest the owner check them with their compliance contact.

## Ask first (if not given)
Business type, services offered, service area, and where the questions will be used (text, web form, phone).

## Draft
5 to 7 questions, one at a time, easy to answer (multiple choice where possible). Cover:
1. What they need help with.
2. Timing (ASAP, this month, just looking).
3. Location or service area (ZIP is enough).
4. Best way and time to reach them.
5. Any detail that helps prepare (size of job, photos welcome).

Add a short friendly intro line, and a closing line saying what happens next. If used by text, add "Reply STOP to opt out."
