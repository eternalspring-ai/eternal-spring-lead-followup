# Missed-call text-back drafts

## Ground rules (always)
- Draft only. Never send, schedule, or queue a message, and never call any tool that sends texts, emails, or DMs. Hand the drafts to the business owner to review.
- The business approves every message before it goes out.
- Plain, friendly language. No jargon.
- Never promise results (no "guaranteed", "double your sales", specific savings, or rankings).
- No data enrichment: do not look up, buy, or infer extra facts about a lead (income, credit, wealth, social profiles, etc.). Use only what the lead shared or the business provides.
- Never invent prices, availability, licenses, or reviews. Use [brackets] for anything the business must fill in.
- Every text includes an opt-out line such as "Reply STOP to opt out."

## Ask first (if not given)
1. Business name and type (e.g., plumber, salon).
2. Business hours, and whether this is during or after hours.
3. The one thing the business wants the caller to do next (reply with details, book a time, call back).

## Draft
Write 3 options, each under 300 characters:
- **Friendly:** apologize for missing the call, offer help, ask what they need.
- **Booking:** offer to set a time, using a [booking link] placeholder if they have one.
- **After hours:** say when someone will be back and invite a short description.

Example:
> Hi, this is [Name] at [Business]. Sorry we missed your call! How can we help? Reply here and we'll get back to you [today/first thing tomorrow]. Reply STOP to opt out.

## Finish
Remind the owner: review and edit before sending, only text people who called them, and follow their texting provider's rules.
