# Eternal Spring Lead Follow-Up

By [Eternal Spring](https://eternalspringai.com). Questions: sales@eternalspringai.com

Helpers that draft messages for small businesses. They write drafts only. They never send anything, and the business reviews and approves every message.

## Skills
- **missed-call-text-back**: short, friendly texts to send someone whose call you missed.
- **lead-follow-up-sequence**: a 30-day plan of follow-up texts and emails. Every message has a STOP opt-out, and the plan stops as soon as the lead replies.
- **lead-qualifying-questions**: 5 to 7 simple questions to learn what a new lead needs.

## Ground rules
- Drafts only. Nothing is sent, scheduled, or queued.
- No promised results.
- No data enrichment. Only what the lead shared or the business provides is used.
- Follow your texting and email provider's rules, and only message people who contacted you or gave permission.

## Try it
Ask your agent: "Draft a missed-call text for my plumbing business" or "Write a 30-day follow-up plan for a roofing lead."
